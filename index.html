            About Me
            

              A developer who cares about the whole picture
            

          


          

            

              

                I'm Syed Fahad Ali, a Full Stack Developer from India with a
                passion for building modern, scalable, and high-performance web

                designing intuitive user interfaces, building reusable frontend

                that keep everything running reliably.

                using.
